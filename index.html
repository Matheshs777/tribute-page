Crisitiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo is one of the greatest footballers in history.

Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese professional footballer and the greatest goal-scorer in the history of the sport. Born in Madeira, he moved to Lisbon at age 12 to join the Sporting CP academy. After catching Manchester United's eye with a stunning pre-season performance in 2003, he went on to conquer England, Spain, Italy, and Saudi Arabia — becoming the first player to win league titles and Golden Boots in four different countries. Now 40 years old and still competing at the highest level, he has confirmed the 2026 FIFA World Cup will be his final international tournament.
